This creates an experience that looks different from traditional computer-generated online games.

A simple way to understand the technology is:

**Live Studio or Venue → Video Stream → Digital Interface → User's Device**

This guide explains what live casino means, what a live dealer is, how the technology works, and how live games differ from regular online casino-style games.

## Why Is There a Delay in Live Streaming?

Real-time internet video is not always literally instantaneous.

A small delay can occur because the video must travel through several technical stages before appearing on a user's device.

These can include:

**Capture → Processing → Transmission → Internet Connection → Device Playback**

Streaming services generally try to keep this delay low.

However, connection quality, device performance, network conditions, and streaming technology can affect the experience.

This is not unique to live casino streaming.

Similar delays occur with sports streams, live broadcasts, and video conferencing.
